How to Remove Passwords from Bank & Salary PDFs Safely

Tired of typing your date of birth or PAN number every single time you open monthly salary payslips or credit card e-statements? Learn how to remove owner passwords locally without exposing credentials to remote cloud services.

Why Password Removal Demands Local Processing

Banks and financial institutions protect e-statements with user passwords (typically combinations of DOB, SSN, or account digits).

When you submit a password-protected PDF to an online unlocking service, you must type both your secret password and upload the encrypted file. If that service logs inputs, the provider receives both the lock and the key to your bank account records!
