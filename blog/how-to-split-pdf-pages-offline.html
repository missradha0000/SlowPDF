How to Split & Extract Specific PDF Pages 100% Offline

Need to isolate page 14 of a 200-page lease or extract signature exhibits from a corporate contract? Learn how to burst, split, and re-bundle pages locally in your browser.

Why Extracting Pages Locally Matters

When working with confidential legal agreements, HR employee handbooks, or multi-year financial audits, you often need to share only 1 or 2 specific pages with external vendors or clients. Sending the entire 100-page document risks exposing sensitive annexures or private salary clauses.

SlowPDF's Split PDF Tool parses the PDF object tree in RAM and renders clear page thumbnail cards. You simply click the exact pages you want to extract or specify a custom range (e.g. 1, 3-7, 12).

Common Page Extraction Use Cases

  • Legal Agreements: Extracting signature pages for counterpart execution.
  • University Textbooks: Saving specific chapter reading assignments for offline review.
  • Tax Documentation: Extracting Schedules C or W-2 attachments from large multi-year returns.
