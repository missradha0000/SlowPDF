Common PDF Errors & How to Fix Them in Your Browser

Ever encountered "The document is damaged and cannot be repaired" or weird garbled characters when opening a PDF? Learn the top 4 PDF structure errors and how to repair them in-browser.

Top 4 PDF Rendering Errors

1. Corrupt XREF Table (Cross-Reference Byte Offset Error)

Occurs when incomplete downloads truncate byte ranges. Solution: Re-save the document through SlowPDF's client-side WASM parser, which rebuilds the XREF catalog from scratch.

2. Missing Embedded Font Glyphs (Garbled Text)

When custom fonts are not embedded. Solution: Convert PDF pages to high-resolution images or flattened raster layers.

3. Invalid MediaBox & CropBox Dimensions

Causes pages to appear rotated or partially cut off when printing. Solution: Use SlowPDF Crop or Rotate tools to standardize viewport boxes.
