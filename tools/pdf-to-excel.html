How to Convert PDF to Excel Spreadsheet (.XLSX) Online Free

Extracting tables from PDF documents into editable Excel spreadsheets is one of the most critical daily workflows for accountants, data analysts, students, and business owners. Whether working with bank statements, tax documents, invoice ledgers, or scientific data tables, re-keying numbers manually is time-consuming and error-prone.

SlowPDF solves this problem with 100% client-side WebAssembly technology. By combining PDF position coordinate parsing with SheetJS data structures, your browser automatically aligns rows and columns into clean, formula-ready Microsoft Excel spreadsheets—all without sending a single byte to an external cloud server.

Simple 3-Step Conversion Walkthrough:

1

Select PDF File

Drag and drop any PDF containing tables, lists, or financial data into the workbench zone.

2

Configure & Preview

Choose whether to combine all pages into one main sheet or separate sheets per page. Inspect the live row/column preview.

3

Download .XLSX

Click "Convert to Excel". Save your native .xlsx spreadsheet directly to your device with instant formatting.
