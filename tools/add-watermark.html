Free Online PDF Watermark Tool — Protect Documents with Text & Logos

Digital documents and sensitive PDFs can easily be leaked, misattributed, or shared without permission. With SlowPDF Add Watermark to PDF, you can apply custom text stamps (like CONFIDENTIAL, DRAFT, DO NOT SHARE) or company logo watermarks with adjustable transparency, diagonal rotation, and dense repeating tile patterns.

1 How to Add Watermarks to PDF in 3 Easy Steps

  1. Upload PDF Document: Select or drop your multi-page PDF into the upload area.
  2. Customize Watermark: Switch between Text or Logo mode, type your watermark or upload a logo image, adjust opacity, rotation angle, font size, and pattern mode. Watch the live continuous reader update instantly.
  3. Download Watermarked PDF: Click "Apply Watermark & Download PDF". SlowPDF vector embeds your watermark layers inside browser local RAM using WebAssembly.

Why SlowPDF is the Best Private Watermarking Tool

Unlike web services that transmit confidential legal contracts, business plans, or tax records to external cloud servers, SlowPDF executes 100% of PDF processing inside local browser RAM using pdf-lib and WebAssembly.

  • 100% Client-Side Privacy: Your sensitive files and logos never leave your physical device.
  • Dense Tile Grid Defense: Tile watermarks repeatedly across pages to prevent cropping or unauthorized camera capture.
  • Layer Control: Choose whether to stamp watermarks over content or behind existing page layers.
  • Zero Vector Loss: Document typography, form fields, and resolution remain 100% intact.
