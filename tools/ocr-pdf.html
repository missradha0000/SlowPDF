Complete Guide to OCR PDF

Fast, private & free client-side PDF utility.

SlowPDF provides professional-grade PDF utilities that execute 100% locally inside your web browser. Utilizing modern WebAssembly and modern JavaScript APIs, your files are processed in local device memory without ever touching remote cloud servers.

Why Choose Zero-Server Client-Side Execution?

Traditional PDF websites upload your files to remote storage buckets, creating privacy hazards for sensitive documents like tax returns, transcripts, contracts, and personal records. SlowPDF guarantees complete privacy because data streams remain strictly inside your device's RAM sandbox.
