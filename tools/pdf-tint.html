Print & Ink-Saver Mode: Convert Dark PDFs to Pure White Backgrounds

Downloading dark-mode slide decks, black-background PDF ebooks, or dark smartphone scans poses a major challenge when you need to print or photocopy physical pages. Printing a dark background PDF wastes massive amounts of printer ink and black toner cartridge, resulting in soggy paper, long print delays, and illegible text.

SlowPDF's "Invert to White (Print/Ink-Saver)" preset converts black and dark gray backgrounds to pure crisp white while transforming white or light text into jet-black lettering. Our Background Whiteness slider further removes camera shadows, lighting artifacts, and paper creases from phone-scanned lecture notes, saving up to 90% of printer ink during physical printing.

Comfortable Night Study & Eye-Care Modes (Warm Sepia & Green)

Reading bright white PDF documents on mobile screens or laptops in dim lighting causes severe digital eye strain, headaches, and sleep disruption. SlowPDF provides dedicated eye-care reading presets:

  • Dark Mode (Night Reading): Converts bright white documents into OLED black backgrounds with soft white text for zero glare in dark rooms.
