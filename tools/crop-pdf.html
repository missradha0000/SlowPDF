Why Crop PDF Margins Client-Side with Vector Accuracy?

Standard online PDF cropping tools often rasterize document pages into low-resolution images, causing blurry text, loss of vector sharpness, and bloated file sizes. SlowPDF takes a superior approach by modifying internal PDF CropBox and MediaBox viewport definitions directly via PDF-Lib in browser RAM.

By adjusting viewport vectors directly, your document maintains 100% of its original vector clarity, embedded fonts, high-resolution graphics, and selectable text lines.

100% Private Client-Side Security

Cropping sensitive business papers, financial reports, or academic manuscripts requires maximum privacy. SlowPDF performs 100% of rendering, margin detection, and PDF generation locally inside your device's memory. Zero data ever leaves your computer or phone.
