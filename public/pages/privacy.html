Privacy Policy

Our strict zero-server privacy architecture: your files never upload to any remote server. Ever.

1. Core Philosophy

At SlowPDF (slowpdf.in), we believe the modern internet has grown overwhelmingly noisy and rushed. Tools that should take 3 seconds now demand user registrations, email sign-ups, subscriptions, and invasive cloud storage uploads. We built SlowPDF to be the antidote: a calm, steady, reliable study partner that respects your focus and your device.

2. Zero Server Architecture

Every tool on SlowPDF operates client-side. When you select or drag a document, it is read into your browser's local sandbox memory (RAM) via standard WebAssembly and Web Worker APIs. The calculations happen directly on your CPU or GPU. Nothing is transmitted over the network to any third-party or server.

3. Built For Deep Learners

Whether you are a medical student highlighting 800-page textbooks, an academic researcher compiling multi-source literature, or a legal professional reviewing contracts, you deserve tools that operate without distraction, timers, or privacy hazards.
