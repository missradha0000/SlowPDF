Why Convert Color PDF Documents to Black & White?

Printing color PDF files on standard office printers rapidly drains expensive color ink cartridges. Converting your files to grayscale or high-contrast black and white forces printers to use standard monochrome black toner, reducing printing costs by up to 60%.

In addition, grayscale conversion reduces file size, strips away distracting background gradients, and enhances legibility when making physical paper photocopies.
