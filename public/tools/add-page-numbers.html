Free Online PDF Page Numbering — Add Custom Header & Footer Numbers

Adding continuous page numbers, headers, or footers to PDF files is essential when compiling academic research papers, corporate reports, court filings, and published ebooks. With SlowPDF Add Page Numbers, you can easily stamp custom pagination onto your documents directly inside your browser memory with zero quality degradation and complete privacy.

1 How to Add Page Numbers to PDF in 3 Easy Steps

  1. Upload PDF: Drag and drop your multi-page PDF document into the SlowPDF upload box.
  2. Select Visual Position & Format: Click any of the 6 hotspot zones on our interactive page mockup (Top-Left, Top-Center, Top-Right, Bottom-Left, Bottom-Center, Bottom-Right) and choose your preferred font, color, and number format.
  3. Download Paginated PDF: Click "Apply Page Numbers & Download PDF". SlowPDF stamps clean vector page numbers directly onto your document in local browser RAM.

Why SlowPDF is the Best Private Numbering Tool

Unlike cloud services that upload confidential documents to external servers, SlowPDF calculates exact point coordinates and stamps vector typography completely inside local browser RAM using WebAssembly and pdf-lib.

  • 100% Client-Side Privacy: Your sensitive financial statements and legal filings never touch remote servers.
  • Precision Point Alignment: Calculates text metrics using font.widthOfTextAtSize() for perfect centering and right-edge alignment.
  • Flexible Cover Logic: Easily skip cover or title pages and set starting number offsets.
  • Zero Compression Loss: Preserves original document clarity, embedded images, and vector graphics.
