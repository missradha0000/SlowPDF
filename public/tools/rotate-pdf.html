Free Online PDF Page Rotator — Rotate Single or All Pages Permanently

Scanning contracts, receipts, or academic papers often results in sideways or upside-down pages that disrupt reading and digital archiving. With SlowPDF Rotate PDF, you can instantly turn individual pages or whole multi-page documents to their correct orientation—permanently fixing page angles directly inside your browser.

1 How to Rotate PDF Pages in 3 Simple Steps

  1. Upload Your PDF Document: Drag and drop any multi-page PDF or click "Select PDF Document". Your file is loaded instantly into browser local memory.
  2. Set Orientation Angles: Click the ↺ (Rotate Left) or ↻ (Rotate Right) buttons on individual page thumbnail cards, or use "Rotate All" to turn the entire document 90°, 180°, or 270°.
  3. Download Your Permanent PDF: Click "Save & Download PDF". SlowPDF updates the native page metadata tags using client-side JavaScript and generates a fresh, clean PDF file ready for use.

Why SlowPDF is the Best Tool for Rotating Documents

Unlike cloud-based PDF tools that upload confidential contracts to external servers, SlowPDF executes 100% of page rendering and rotation inside your device's browser RAM using pdf-lib and PDF.js. No files leave your device, ensuring total security and zero data exposure.

  • Permanent Metadata Fix: Updates native PDF rotation tags so pages open correctly in Adobe Acrobat, Google Drive, and mobile readers.
  • Zero Quality Loss: Preserves original vector artwork, crisp typography, hyperlinks, and embedded images without re-compression.
  • 100% In-Browser RAM Security: Your confidential tax filings, medical reports, and legal contracts stay 100% local.
  • No Caps & Offline Ready: Process large multi-page PDF files smoothly without paywalls, sign-ups, or limits.
